# CED Explorer - Write-In Edition

An interactive Cherokee language learning tool based on the Cherokee English Dictionary (CED).

## Overview

Unlike multiple-choice mode, the Write-in Edition prompts you to produce the requested grammatical form of a verb seen/heard in a full example sentence.

1. **See and Hear**: Read the sentence in Cherokee Syllabary and phonetics, with audio playback (when available).
2. **Target Form**: Look at the specified target form to produce (e.g. *Third person present*, *First person present*, *Third person completive past*, *Third person habitual*, *Second person imperative*, *Third person infinitive*).
3. **Type Response**: Enter the form without needing to type tone numbers or special tone marks (e.g., type `gadadega` for `ga.²da.²de³ga`). Syllabary input is also accepted.
4. **Immediate Feedback & CED Links**: Check your answer, view the formatted tone notation, read the English sentence translation, and link directly to the entry on CherokeeDictionary.net.
